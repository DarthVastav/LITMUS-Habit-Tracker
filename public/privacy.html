Litmus — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026 · Litmus is a personal student wellness & productivity tracker.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and name (typed in, or provided by Google if you sign in with Google).
  • What you track: your tasks, habits and check-ins, assignments, exam-prep topics, course selections, focus-session durations, and settings like theme and reminder preferences.
  • Shared timetable data: class schedules, exam schedules and mess menus uploaded by users are visible to all users of the app — they describe the institute, not any person.
  • Reliability data: if the app crashes, it reports the technical error (message and code location — never the content of your tasks or habits). Push reminders store a device notification token.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no analytics SDKs, no fingerprinting.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No location tracking, no contact access.

Where it lives

Data is stored in Supabase (hosted Postgres) and the app is served via Cloudflare. Your personal data is protected by row-level security: only your signed-in account can read it. A copy of your data is cached on your own device so the app works offline.

Your controls

  • Reset all data in Settings erases everything you track, immediately, on the server too.
  • For full account deletion or a copy of your data, email us and we'll do it promptly.
